# Part 31: The Age Beyond Engineering

## Chapter 1: The New Frontier — Exploring What Comes After Creation

The team had completed the Age of Engineering.

They had explored:

* Tools and machines
* Systems and intelligence
* Civilizations and realities
* Knowledge and meaning

They had reached the realization that engineering was humanity's method of transforming imagination into reality.

But Sarah introduced a new question.

She entered the engineering room and wrote:

**"When everything can be engineered, what becomes the next frontier?"**

---

Emma looked at the statement.
